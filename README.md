# BrightWire News — Curated Positive Journalism & Free Daily AI Tools

**[BrightWire News (brightwire.news)](https://www.brightwire.news)** is an independent constructive journalism platform that filters 300+ verified global sources for real breakthroughs in science, medicine, clean energy, conservation, technology, and human kindness—without outrage or doomscrolling.

## Core Editorial Features
- **[BrightWire Homepage](https://www.brightwire.news)** — Daily curated positive journalism from 300+ global publications.
- **[Today's Positive News Briefing](https://www.brightwire.news/today)** — Listen to or read today's top verified uplifting stories with natural UK & US neural audio narration.
- **[Good News Topics Hub](https://www.brightwire.news/good-news)** — Deep-dive hubs across renewable energy, medical breakthroughs, wildlife recovery, and community heroes.
- **[On This Day in Positive History](https://www.brightwire.news/on-this-day)** — Historical milestones of human progress for every day of the year.
